How it reads the herd

Prototype experience · demo data · not a diagnostic tool

01 / The signal field

The early clue is rarely in one place.

Farm teams already notice hundreds of small changes. The system gives those observations a shared memory, a time horizon, and a reason to look closer.

Milk pattern

Conductivity and yield changes, read as a trend rather than a single spike.

Movement

Rest, steps, feeding visits, and a quiet change in daily rhythm.

Temperature

A local heat pattern that adds context to the other observations.

Farm context

Weather, bedding, pen, lactation stage, and milking conditions.

02 / From noise to action

A risk window, not a black box.

Every watch comes with the small set of signals that moved it, so a farmer, veterinarian, or cooperative lead can decide what deserves attention.

01 — notice

Signals converge

Milk, motion, temperature, and context are aligned against that animal’s own rhythm.

02 — explain

Risk surfaces early

Animals move into Low, Watch, or Elevated categories with a visible reason, not just a score.

03 — respond

Teams choose the next step

Check bedding, observe the next milking, or call a veterinarian. Prevention stays human-led.

03 / Built for the field

Trust is a feature in the shed.

Designed for uneven connectivity, multiple roles, and the judgement that only a person who knows the herd can bring.

Explain before you escalate.

Risk categories are prompts for observation and professional judgement. The prototype intentionally avoids presenting a clinical diagnosis.

Made for prevention.

Small, practical next steps fit the rhythm of milking, bedding checks, and veterinary rounds.

One herd, many hands.

Farmers, vets, cooperatives, and health authorities can share the same readable picture.
